Radix overlays (dialogs, menus, popovers) animate with `tw-animate-css` (`animate-in`, `fade-in`, `zoom-in-95`, …), which ships with the stylesheet.

## Reduced motion

The stylesheet collapses every animation and transition to near zero under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. You do not have to opt in, but you must not fight it: never encode meaning only in motion.
