# Elevation

Depth comes from three things: the surface ramp (`canvas` → `background` → `card` → `popover`), 1px borders, and three shadows. Shadows get four times stronger in dark mode, where faint ones would vanish.

## Surface utilities

<SurfaceUtilities />

- **`card-surface`** is the whole card recipe in one class: border, `rounded-card`, `bg-card` and `shadow-soft`.
- **`glass`** is for sticky headers, toolbars and floating bars sitting over scrolling content. Not for cards at rest.
