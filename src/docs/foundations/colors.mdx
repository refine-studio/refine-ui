# Colours

Every colour is OKLCH and every surface uses a semantic token. Switch **Mode** in the toolbar and the swatches follow.

## Neutrals

Zinc, exactly as on refine.studio. `canvas` sits behind everything, and `background`, `card` and `popover` stack on top.

## Status

Status colours are constant and never follow the accent. Each family has a base for fills and icons, a foreground for text on that fill, a soft for backgrounds, and an ink for text on soft or on any surface. Every foreground passes 4.5:1 on its fill in both modes.

Orange sits between warning and danger, for a severity that is worse than a warning but not yet an error.

Violet carries no severity. Use it for a state that is neither good nor bad, such as an early stage, where teal would read as the brand accent.

## Lifecycle

Four colours for where a thing is in its life. They alias the status tones and the zinc neutrals, and add no hue of their own. An end of life is a quiet grey, never amber or red: red stays for risk, churn and incidents.

## Charts

Eight colours for the series of one chart. They are the same in light and dark mode. Take them in order from `chart-1`, so a chart with three series uses the first three. Beyond eight series, group the smallest into **Other** in `muted-foreground`.

**A chart colour never looks like a status or like the brand.** Every chart hue keeps at least 25° in OKLCH from success, warning, danger, info and the gem teal, in both modes. Only five hues are that far away: raspberry, violet, magenta, sky and olive. Violet, sky and olive come twice, as a light and a dark step. Every pair stays apart for readers with protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia. To colour by status, use the status or lifecycle tokens, not a chart colour.

**Every chart carries a legend or direct labels, plus a tooltip.** The fills reach 2:1 on every surface, not 3:1. WCAG 1.4.11 allows that only when the colour is not the sole way to read the data.
