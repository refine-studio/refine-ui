# Colours

Every colour is OKLCH and every surface uses a semantic token. Switch **Mode** in the toolbar and the swatches follow.

## Neutrals

Zinc, exactly as on refine.studio. `canvas` sits behind everything, and `background`, `card` and `popover` stack on top.

## Accent: refine teal

The accent is the refine teal. Its utilities carry the name **gem**: `bg-gem`, `text-gem-ink`, `bg-gem-soft`, the gradients and the `gem-50` to `gem-950` ramp.

## Status

Status colours are constant and never follow the accent. Each family has a base for fills and icons, a foreground for text on that fill, a soft for backgrounds, and an ink for text on soft or on any surface. Every foreground passes 4.5:1 on its fill in both modes.

Orange sits between warning and danger, for a severity that is worse than a warning but not yet an error.

Violet carries no severity. Use it for a state that is neither good nor bad, such as an early stage, where teal would read as the brand accent.

## Lifecycle

Four colours for where a thing is in its life. They alias the status tones and the zinc neutrals, and add no hue of their own. An end of life is a quiet grey, never amber or red: red stays for risk, churn and incidents.

## Charts

**Categories use the primary ramp.** `chart-1` to `chart-4` are one ramp of the gem hue, strongest first. Dark mode has its own brighter steps, so the tail stays apart from the dark card. A single series takes `chart-1`. Beyond four categories, group the rest into **Others** in `chart-other`. Context lines in a line chart take `chart-other-line`, a step stronger, because a thin stroke needs more contrast than a band.

**Lifecycle uses the `status-*` tokens.** To do, in progress, done and ended never take a chart colour.

**Good and bad use `success` and `danger`.** A gain or a loss is a status, not a category.

Every chart carries a legend or direct labels, plus a tooltip. Every step keeps 2:1 on card and canvas, which WCAG 1.4.11 allows only when the colour is not the sole way to read the data. The two neutrals are quieter on purpose and keep at least 1.3:1 on card and canvas.
