# Colours

Every colour is OKLCH and every surface uses a semantic token. Switch **Mode** in the toolbar and the swatches follow.

## Neutrals

Zinc, exactly as on refine.studio. `canvas` sits behind everything, and `background`, `card` and `popover` stack on top.

## Accent: refine teal

The accent is the refine teal. Its utilities carry the name **gem**: `bg-gem`, `text-gem-ink`, `bg-gem-soft`, the gradients and the `gem-50` to `gem-950` ramp.

## Status

Status colours are constant and never follow the accent. Each family has a base for fills and icons, a foreground for text on that fill, a soft for backgrounds, and an ink for text on soft or on any surface. Every foreground passes 4.5:1 on its fill in both modes.

Orange sits between warning and danger, for a severity that is worse than a warning but not yet an error.

Violet carries no severity. Use it for a state that is neither good nor bad, such as an early stage, where teal would read as the brand accent.

## Interaction overlays

Hover and pressed states are translucent layers of the current foreground, so they work on any surface. `scrim` sits behind dialogs and sheets.
